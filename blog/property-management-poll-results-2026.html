21 Polls Later: What Property Managers Told Me in 2026

When I launched the Tuesday Edition of my newsletter, I added a poll to almost every other issue. The idea was simple: give the industry current benchmarks, straight from operators, instead of guessing what everyone else is doing.

The first poll asked what you thought of a Tuesday newsletter at all. 84% of you said you loved it. (A brave 4% said “I hate it. Never do another one. Seriously.” Sorry, folks.)

Twenty more polls and over 2,000 votes later, this is the full set of property management poll results from 2026 in one place: the numbers, my takes, and the best comments you left along the way. I grouped them by topic.

The year in four numbers

60%switched PM software and are glad they did
79%call an Open API a deal-breaker or very important
57%handle trust accounting in-house
67%view NARPM favorably

Your tech stack

Which PM accounting software do you use? (April 2026)

Wow, this genuinely surprised me. AppFolio still leads with half of the 212 votes, but Rentvine has passed Buildium among my readers and now sits in second place.

I plan to run this one about once a year to track the trend.

Have you ever switched PM software? (June 2026)

60% of you have switched at least once and are glad you did. Only 3% switched and wish they hadn’t. This one drew tons of great feedback:

“Switches are never fun and no software is perfect. It’s important to understand what the grass looks like on the other side separate and aside from what a salesperson tells you.”

I agree with that one.

“Switched from Rent Manager to Rentvine. Rent Manager wasn’t a bottleneck for us, but switching to new software allowed us to do so much more.”

“Switched after a disastrous DRE audit in 2016 from a very old version of Yardi Genesis, to AppFolio and have never looked back. AppFolio is super expensive, but it’s a cost of doing business and we have gone from almost going out of business with 200 units to thriving with over 600 now.”

And from the camp that’s staying put: “The learning curve is too steep to change.”

We’re at 750 doors now and everyone keeps asking me when we’re going to switch off Buildium. My answer: when the software becomes the bottleneck to hitting our business goals.

Which software do you use for maintenance coordination? (May 2026)

49% of respondents use the tools built into their PM software for maintenance coordination. Property Meld is the top point solution at 26%. Vendoroo, Haven AI, and Mason each picked up a handful of votes, and a few of you are running homemade or vibe-coded tools.

One long-time Meld user is getting restless:

“I’ve been with Property Meld for 8 years, but I’m starting to shop around for something different, perhaps with a more streamlined AI strategy to better accommodate small operators like me.”

How important is an Open API? (May 2026)

43% called an Open API a deal-breaker, and another 36% said it’s very important. An Open API is no longer optional for property management software vendors.

(Vendors: you can see how yours stacks up on my API Report Card.)

Platform tools or point solutions? (October 2026)

This one’s a coin flip. “A mix of both, leaning toward my platform” got the most votes (37%), with best-in-class point solutions right behind (33%). Add up each side and you get a dead heat: half of you lean toward your platform, half lean toward point solutions.

Which email service do you use? (June 2026)

Google Workspace won with 54%, and Microsoft 365 took 34%. I use Google Workspace at RL. One Microsoft fan said that when they moved their team off Google Workspace, “they were thrilled.” Another reader runs everything through Superhuman: “Gmail and Outlook (3 accounts to 1).”

AI

What’s your AI daily driver? (March 2026)

Most property managers are still using ChatGPT (for now): 41% to Claude’s 27%, with Gemini a distant third.

Back in March I predicted Claude would take the lead within six months, given how fast Anthropic was shipping new Cowork features every week. Six months have come and gone. Time to rerun this one.

Are PM software vendors doing a great job implementing AI? (November 2026)

You’re split. 40% agree, 36% disagree, and about a quarter of you are on the fence. The comments explain why:

“I have yet to see any major time savers introduced. I’m hopeful that that will change in the near future.” (Brian W.)

“They are implementing it but it does not usually answer the question I am asking and I end up going to a live person for support anyway.” (Hearthside Rentals, NC)

“It’s all software - and vendor-specific.” (Sean B.)

And one reader skipped the debate:

“I’m more focused on building my own AI agents than relying on vendor-provided AI. What matters more to me is access to the data inside these systems so my AI agents can use it.”

That’s the Open API poll all over again.

How you run the company

How is your PM company organized? (February 2026)

Hybrid is the clear winner at 40%, ahead of pods (about a quarter). The departmental structure is falling out of favor.

How do you handle trust accounting? (March 2026)

This surprised me: 57% of you keep trust accounting in-house. I guess a lot of you have great accounting skills! Another 23% outsource it and 17% run a hybrid.

If you’d like an introduction to the group we use for trust accounting, let me know. They’ve done a really great job for us for years now.

How do you get rental listing photos? (August 2026)

35% shoot photos in-house, 28% hire a local photographer, and 18% use PlanOmatic. The in-house crowd had the most to say:

“We use Ricoh360 which makes HD photos and a virtual tour very easy and quick.”

“Owners can pay additional for us to hire a professional photographer. Less than 1% of our owners opt in for this paid service.”

Is your use of global talent changing? (July 2026)

Property managers are still using global talent, and most of you are holding steady or hiring more. Only 18% are cutting back. AI agents are not making a big dent (yet). This one sparked a good discussion when I shared it on LinkedIn too.

Do you offer bulk internet to tenants? (September 2026)

Only 17% offer it today, but more than half of you are considering it. The comments showed both sides:

“In my area (Atlanta suburb) 1 Gig is $90 to $100 so at a $65 charge, including markup, it’s a savings.” (Tal K.)

“Not interested in providing IT support as a part of PM” (Chad H.)

How much time goes to city rental registries? (February 2026)

43% of you spend zero hours on rental registries (or don’t have one to deal with). But 15% spend more than 20 hours a month. That’s a part-time job.

A lot of us are forced to waste time fussing with city rental registries, and it’s getting worse. I hate rental registries and think we should fight back with model legislation of our own.

Business health

How’s your client churn compared to last year? (January 2026)

45% of you said churn was lower, either much lower or somewhat lower. My own churn was down in 2025, and it looks like a lot of you saw the same thing. Good news for property managers.

NARPM and conferences

How favorable is your view of NARPM? (October 2026)

Turns out NARPM is VERY popular with property managers! 67% of you view it favorably and fewer than 1 in 10 view it unfavorably. That’s the opposite of how a lot of real estate agents feel about NAR.

“I was devastated when the organization I had come to love made the choice to dissolve the Women’s Council.” (Ashley A., who still thinks NARPM is worth the time and money)

“Platform feels outdated, logging in is not easy across devices, needs more member participation.” (Miranda W.)

“It has been an amazing source of information to help me better run my PM business.” (Jamie H.)

Do you read NARPM’s magazine? (August 2026)

Half of you are NARPM members who don’t read Residential Resource. About a third read it most months, with digital readers outnumbering print.

Are you going to NARPM Broker/Owner? (April 2026)

54% of you said yes. If you were one of them, I hope you found me and said hi.

Which fall conference are you most excited about? (June 2026)

Looks like AppFolio FUTURE and NARPM National are the clear leaders here. A bunch of people wanted to vote for the PM Systems Conference, which technically isn’t until January.

Just for fun

What’s your favorite ice cream? (September 2026)

Vanilla wins. I’m a Vanilla man myself. Moose Tracks deserves an honorable mention from the “other” comments.

What’s next

I’ll keep running these, and I’ll repeat the software and AI polls so we can see what changes from year to year.
